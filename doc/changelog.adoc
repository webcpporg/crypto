== Changelog

What changed in crypto in each version of webcpp, newest first, one line
per change. "Unreleased" is what the next version brings. The release notes
of every version are on webcpp's index page.

[#crypto-changelog-unreleased]
=== Unreleased

// tag::unreleased[]
* Added crypto, primitives over C backends: ciphers, curves and randomness, errors of fixed values in webcpp.crypto, every result `+[[nodiscard]]+`, and WEBCPP_CRYPTO_HAS_THREADS, defined natively and with Emscripten's -pthread unless WEBCPP_CRYPTO_NO_THREADS is.
* Added equal, random_device, pem (PEM on libsodium), ciphers::aes128, aes192 and aes256, the AES block cipher, and ciphers::gcm, aes128_gcm and aes256_gcm: AES-GCM in place, incremental seal, failed opens zeroing data.
* Added supports_v with its family tags, the capability table per target, default_backend_t and /webcpp/crypto//<family>-default, one default per family and target, and refusals naming their Jamfile line.
* Added backends::bearssl (AES-GCM alone, allocating nothing), backends::openssl via /webcpp/crypto//openssl natively and on WASI (allocating at make only, or natively in openssl_memory_resource with WEBCPP_CRYPTO_OPENSSL_MEMORY_RESOURCE), and backends::ctaes (block cipher alone).
* Added curves::secp256k1: key pairs, BIP340 and ECDSA signatures, ECDH, strict DER, low-S and the x-only tweak.
* Added curves::ed25519 and curves::x25519 on libsodium: strict verification over twelve edge cases, Ed25519ph over hash's sha2_512 (/webcpp/crypto//crypto brings hash), the conversion, all-zero secrets refused, and RFC 8410's SPKI and PKCS#8.
* Added backends::sodium on /third_party//sodium, shared with webcpp::hash, every backend's link-time canary and C suite, and ciphers::aead: chacha20_poly1305 (also BearSSL, OpenSSL), its original form and xchacha20_poly1305.
* Added ciphers::secretbox and secretbox_xchacha20poly1305, without additional data, hybrid::box, box_xchacha20poly1305 and sealed_box, and curves::x25519::kx_keypair (crypto_kx) on libsodium, refusing peer keys of small order.
* Added the page: a chapter per namespace and use case, the memory option's guide, tables generated from the tests, every Jamfile and message, llms.txt, and dated release-built sizes and throughput.
* Added secret<N>, a self-wiping array that is copied only by clone, and the errors 13 to 20: memory_functions_refused, memory_functions_not_installed, weak_public_key, invalid_point, invalid_scalar, invalid_padding, invalid_encoding and invalid_ciphertext_size.
// end::unreleased[]
