[#crypto-secp256k1]
== secp256k1

`<webcpp/crypto/secp256k1.hpp>` is secp256k1 on libsecp256k1
(`/third_party//secp256k1`, <<crypto-backends-secp256k1>>), in the namespace
`webcpp::crypto::secp256k1`. A `keypair` is made from a 32-byte secret key with
`make(secret)`, which allocates its signing context, the one allocation, in the
resource it is given. A key pair gives its BIP340 x-only and its compressed
public key, signs with BIP340 Schnorr (`sign_schnorr`) or ECDSA over a 32-byte
hash (`sign_ecdsa`, RFC 6979's nonce, low-S, compact form), derives an ECDH
secret (`ecdh`) and takes BIP341's Taproot tweak (`tweak_taproot`).
`verify_schnorr` and `verify_ecdsa` check signatures; `to_der`, `from_der`,
`from_lax_der` and `normalize_ecdsa` convert ECDSA's DER and low-S forms; and
`taproot_output_key` gives the BIP341 output key of an internal key.

The Bitcoin example signs a native P2WPKH input of BIP143's published
transaction: it computes the BIP143 sighash with Boost.Hash2's SHA-256, signs it
with ECDSA, encodes the signature as strict DER, checks both against BIP143's,
and prints the input's witness.
