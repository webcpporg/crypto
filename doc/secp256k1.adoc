[#crypto-secp256k1]
== secp256k1

`<webcpp/crypto/curves/secp256k1.hpp>` is secp256k1 on libsecp256k1
(`/third_party//secp256k1`, <<crypto-backends-secp256k1>>), in the namespace
`webcpp::crypto::curves::secp256k1`. A `keypair` is made from a 32-byte secret
key with `make(secret)`, which allocates its signing context, the one
allocation, in the resource it is given. A key pair gives its BIP340 x-only and
its compressed public key, signs with BIP340 Schnorr (`sign_schnorr`) or ECDSA
over a 32-byte hash (`sign_ecdsa`, RFC 6979's nonce, low-S, compact form),
derives an ECDH secret (`ecdh`) and is tweaked in place by a 32-byte tweak
(`tweak_xonly`). `verify_schnorr` and `verify_ecdsa` check signatures; `to_der`,
`from_der` (strict DER) and `normalize_ecdsa` convert ECDSA's DER and low-S
forms; and `tweak_xonly` tweaks an x-only public key, giving the tweaked key and
the parity of its point.

A tweak is a primitive: crypto does not compute one. A protocol that defines a
tweak (BIP341's Taproot output key, for one) belongs to a domain library, which
computes the tweak from its own hash and passes it here.

The example signs BIP340's first test vector and verifies it, then tweaks the
key pair and signs and verifies again under the tweaked key.
