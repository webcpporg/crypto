[#crypto-root]
== The root utilities: `webcpp::crypto`

The namespace `webcpp::crypto` holds what every other chapter relies on: where
secrets come from (`random_device`), how a secret is checked
(`constant_time_equal`) and erased from a buffer of the program's
(`erase_secret`), and how a failure is told apart (`error`), none of which needs
a backend; and how a
key's standard form becomes text and back (`pem`, on libsodium;
<<crypto-key-formats>>). It also declares, for a native program on OpenSSL that
turns the memory option on, the resource every allocation of OpenSSL's goes to
(`openssl_memory_resource`, <<crypto-memory-openssl>>). And it holds what every
curve's signatures share: the schemes, the `prehashed` marker, `crypto::sign`
and `crypto::verify` (`<webcpp/crypto/signing.hpp>`), and the key pair
contract, the concept `crypto::keypair`, with `crypto::agree`
(`<webcpp/crypto/keypair.hpp>`; <<crypto-curves-signatures>>); and the free
forms that make an object by its type, each named by its input,
`crypto::from_secret`, `crypto::from_bytes`, `crypto::from_key` and `crypto::generate`
(`<webcpp/crypto/factories.hpp>`; <<crypto-curves-four-forms>>).
