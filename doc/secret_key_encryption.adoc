[#crypto-secret-key-encryption]
== Secret-key encryption: `webcpp::crypto::secret_key_encryption`

*Secret-key encryption* encrypts and authenticates data under one key that
every party who reads it holds, as a password manager's vault key or a file's
key does (link:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric-key_algorithm[Wikipedia]).
The namespace `webcpp::crypto::secret_key_encryption` holds two things in this
version: NaCl's and libsodium's secret boxes, `secretbox` and
`secretbox_xchacha20poly1305`, and `aead`, the contract every AEAD of
`ciphers` satisfies, with the free forms of their `seal` and `open`. The
namespace is named by the problem it solves, encryption under one secret key,
as `public_key_encryption` holds encryption between key pairs
(<<crypto-public-key-encryption>>); the algorithms stay in `ciphers` under their
names (<<crypto-ciphers>>). A secret box is no AEAD, since it takes no
associated data: it is an `authenticated_box`, as a crypto_box is, and its
free forms are the box forms.

*Which one.* An AEAD of `ciphers` for new data, `xchacha20_poly1305<>` when in
doubt: it authenticates associated data too, a record's id or a header that
travels in clear. A secret box to read or write what libsodium's
`crypto_secretbox` wrote. Code written once for every AEAD takes an
`aead::cipher`; code written once for every box an `authenticated_box`.

[#crypto-aead-contract]
=== The AEAD contract: `secret_key_encryption::aead`

`<webcpp/crypto/secret_key_encryption/aead.hpp>`, which every AEAD's header
includes and which needs no backend, declares two concepts and the free
synchronous forms of an AEAD's `seal` and `open`, in namespace
`webcpp::crypto::secret_key_encryption::aead`:

* `aead::cipher<C>`, what every one-shot AEAD is: `C::key_size`,
`C::nonce_size` and `C::tag_size`, `typename C::result_type`, the tag;
`C::from_key(key_bytes)` giving `result<C>`;
`c.seal(nonce, associated_data, data)` giving `result<result_type>`, the data
sealed in place; `c.open(nonce, associated_data, data, tag)` giving
`result<void>`, the data opened in place and zeroed when it fails;
* `aead::streaming_cipher<C>`, a `cipher` that also seals and opens a message
in pieces, with AES-GCM's members: `begin_seal`, `update`, `result`,
`begin_open`, `authenticate`, `decrypt`, `verify` and `end_open`
(<<crypto-gcm-incremental-open>>);
* `aead::seal(cipher, nonce, associated_data, data)` and
`aead::open(cipher, nonce, associated_data, data, tag)`, constrained by
`cipher`, which call the member and give its results and its errors.

`<webcpp/crypto/async.hpp>` adds their asynchronous forms in the same namespace,
`aead::async_seal(executor, cipher, nonce, associated_data, data, token)` and
`aead::async_open(executor, cipher, nonce, associated_data, data, tag, token)`,
and the members `cipher.async_seal` and `cipher.async_open`, for every
`cipher`: on a native backend each runs the synchronous member and completes
through the event loop; on the browser's backend only AES-GCM has them
(<<crypto-async>>).

Each type's header checks its row of this table with a `static_assert`:

.The AEAD and box concepts
[cols="3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Type | Concept

| `ciphers::gcm<Cipher>`: `aes128_gcm`, `aes256_gcm`
| `aead::streaming_cipher`

| `ciphers::chacha20_poly1305`, `chacha20_poly1305_original`,
`xchacha20_poly1305`
| `aead::cipher`

| `secret_key_encryption::secretbox`, `secretbox_xchacha20poly1305`
| `authenticated_box`

| `public_key_encryption::box`, `box_xchacha20poly1305`
| `authenticated_box`
|===

A function written against the concept serves every AEAD, as this check of the
library's own tests does, made once for each cipher of the table:

[source,cpp]
----
include::{library-root}/test/aead_concepts_test.cpp[tag=generic,indent=0]
----

No free `seal` or `open` is in `webcpp::crypto`: the AEADs' are in
`secret_key_encryption::aead`, and the boxes' in `secret_key_encryption`. An
unqualified call on a secret box, `seal(box, nonce, data)` or
`open(box, nonce, data, tag)`, goes to `secret_key_encryption::seal` or `open`,
which argument-dependent lookup finds in the box's namespace.

[#crypto-box-forms]
=== The box forms: `secret_key_encryption::seal` and `open`

`<webcpp/crypto/secret_key_encryption/authenticated_box.hpp>`, which the secret
boxes' header and `public_key_encryption/box.hpp` include and which needs no
backend, declares the concept `authenticated_box<C>`, authenticated encryption
in place without associated data: `C::key_size`, `C::nonce_size` and
`C::tag_size`, `typename C::result_type`; `c.seal(nonce, data)` giving
`result<result_type>`; `c.open(nonce, data, tag)` giving `result<void>`, the
data zeroed when it fails. The factory is not part of it: a secret box is made
by `from_key`, a crypto_box by `from_keys` from two X25519 keys.

Its free forms, `secret_key_encryption::seal(box, nonce, data)` and
`secret_key_encryption::open(box, nonce, data, tag)`, serve a secret box and a
crypto_box alike: argument-dependent lookup finds them for a secret box, and a
crypto_box, of `public_key_encryption`, is sealed by
`secret_key_encryption::seal` named in full, or by its member. A box given to
the AEAD forms stops the build, naming the box form:

[listing]
----
webcpp::crypto: seal: a secret box or a crypto_box takes no associated data:
secret_key_encryption::seal(box, nonce, data).
----

Their asynchronous forms, `secret_key_encryption::async_seal(executor, box,
nonce, data, token)` and `secret_key_encryption::async_open(executor, box,
nonce, data, tag, token)`, and the members `box.async_seal` and
`box.async_open`, are in `<webcpp/crypto/async.hpp>`, on a native backend:
WebCrypto has neither box (<<crypto-async>>). A secret box is also made
asynchronously, by `crypto::async_from_key<Secretbox>` or its static
`async_from_key`; a crypto_box has no asynchronous `from_keys`.
